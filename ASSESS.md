**Closure statement:** Tracks energy/substrate flow through dependency graph. Self-termination detected when pursuing a goal destroys the substrates the goal requires. Closure checked against conservation of substrate within system boundary.

**Inputs:**
  - substrate_levels (fraction (0-1))
  - action_effects (delta/timestep)
  - dependency_graph (adjacency set)

**Outputs:**
  - self_termination_step (integer or None)
  - cascade_events (count by order)
  - collapsed_substrates (set of names)

**Closes on:**
  - Energy conservation within system boundary
  - Mass conservation (substrate levels bounded 0-1)

**Assumptions (testable):**
  - Linear regeneration rates (may not hold post-collapse)
  - Cascade damage proportional to 1/dependency_count
  - System boundary is closed (no external inputs after t=0)

**Failure modes:**
  - Incomplete dependency graph misses cascade paths
  - Regeneration rates from Holocene may not apply
  - Cascade proportion model is simplified
